![Trust by Design - Digital Identity and SSI](@assets/images/2026_11_02_e-ID.png)

The e-ID is an official proof of identity issued by the state. It is designed to meet the highest standards of security, data protection, and trustworthiness. This presentation will give you an overview of the roles, components and technologies of the [swiyu](https://www.eid.admin.ch/en/swiyu-coming-soon-e) Trust Infrastructure operated by the Federal Government, before taking a closer look at how the state ensures ‘Trust by Design’ from a technological perspective.

The presentation will also outline the importance of developing the infrastructure open-source to further strengthen trust. As the e-ID is just one of many digital credentials that can be issued, stored and verified using the [swiyu](https://www.eid.admin.ch/en/swiyu-coming-soon-e) Trust Infrastructure, the presentation concludes with presenting the ambitions and activities to build an open-source community and flourishing ecosystem of private and public use cases.

The talk will be held in German with some Bernese sprinkled in.

## Speaker

[Alexandra Höss](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexandra-h%C3%B6%C3%9F-4a65aa140/) is responsible for the product and technology strategy of the Swiss e-ID and Trust Infrastructure at the Swiss Federal Office of Justice (FOJ). Before joining FOJ, Alexandra completed her PhD with a research focus on Managing Decentralized Digital Identities at the University of Luxembourg. During her doctoral studies she supported Luxembourg’s Ministry for Digitalisation on its projects related to digital identities.

[André Kunz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andr%C3%A9-kunz-4b937278/) is responsible for Open Source at the Federal Office of Justice. For over 15 years, he has been promoting open technologies, transparency and digital sovereignty. Amongst other things, he is a member of the management board at Puzzle ITC, co-founder of the open-source solution [Hitobito](https://hitobito.com/de/), and a board member of CH Open.
